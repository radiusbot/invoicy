Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Invoicy ("the app") is published by SB ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles. The short version: your business data stays on your device, and we don't collect it.

Your business data stays on your device

Invoices, estimates, clients, expenses, receipts, attachments and your business profile are stored only in a database on your device. We don't operate servers that receive this data, and we can't access it. Backups you export are files that you control; where you save or send them is up to you.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. The app asks the store on your device whether you own an Invoicy Pro plan. We don't run servers that receive your purchase records, and no other company processes them on our behalf. We only see the aggregated, anonymized sales reports that Apple and Google give every developer. We never receive your payment card details.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos: used only when you choose to attach a receipt photo or file. Images stay on your device.
  • Notifications: used only for the invoice due-date reminders you turn on. They are scheduled locally on your device.

Feedback emails

If you use "Send feedback", your email app opens a message to us. It includes the app version and your device's operating system version, which you can see and edit before sending. We use your email only to reply to you.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no tracking SDKs. Diagnostic logs stay on your device.

Children

Invoicy is a business tool and isn't directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all business data stored on the device. Purchase records are held by Apple or Google; manage them through your App Store or Google Play account.

Changes

If our practices change, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date.
