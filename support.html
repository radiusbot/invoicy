Support

We usually reply within two business days.

Contact us

Email radiusbot@gmail.com, or use Settings › Send feedback in the app, which adds your app and OS version for you.

I paid but Pro isn't unlocked

Open the paywall and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. Purchases can take a few minutes to arrive if a parent or guardian has to approve them (Ask to Buy).

Cancel or change a subscription

  • iPhone/iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Invoicy.
  • Android: Google Play › profile icon › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Invoicy.

Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

Move to a new phone

Your data is stored only on your device. On the old phone, go to Settings › Data › Export Backup and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone. On the new phone, install Invoicy and use Settings › Data › Restore from Backup.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds are requested from them: reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or the order in your Google Play order history.
